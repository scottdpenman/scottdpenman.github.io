I’m a multi-disciplinary designer and researcher interested in architecture, emerging tech, and computational models of human creativity. After training as an architect in Mississippi and working on nationally-recognized projects in Arkansas, I came to MIT to pursue a career in computational design and emerging tech research, researching everything from futuristic aquaria to ambient intelligent lighting agents.

In my current role with Sasaki Strategies, I work with data analysts, software developers, and other tech specialists to develop design-focused tools and workflows for all of Sasaki’s design disciplines.
