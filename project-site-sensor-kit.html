Site Sensor Kit

Year
2021
Role
Designer, Engineer
Context
Sasaki Strategies

Design teams routinely make consequential decisions about a site based on a single visit, a handful of photos, and whatever published data exists for the area. The Site Sensor Kit was built to fill the gap between that brief site visit and the longer design development process — providing continuous, multi-dimensional environmental data from a location that would otherwise go unmonitored.

The kit is a deployable IoT system combining sensors for temperature, humidity, light levels, sound, and occupancy into a single ruggedized enclosure that can be installed quickly and left in the field. Data streams continuously to a cloud dashboard, where the design team can visualize environmental conditions across any time period and identify patterns — daily rhythms, seasonal variation, peak usage moments — that inform design decisions with a granularity that a site visit cannot provide.

A site visit tells you what a place looks like. A sensor kit tells you how it behaves.
